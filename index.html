What is BM International?

BM International is a leading B2B garments sourcing agency and textile exporter based in Karachi, Pakistan. Founded by Muhammad Moeen Baig, the company connects global retail brands, apparel wholesalers, and institutional buyers with certified textile manufacturers in Pakistan. We manage the entire supply chain, including supplier vetting, custom sample development, bulk production oversight, on-ground AQL quality inspections, customs clearance, and global shipping.

Who We Serve:

  • Global Apparel Brands: Sourcing custom private-label knitwear, hoodies, denim, flannel jackets, and kids' wear.
  • Textile Importers & Wholesalers: Sourcing bulk bed sheets, hotel towels, institutional workwear, and kitchen linens.
  • Industrial Buyers: Sourcing recycled and new cotton cleaning rags (wipers) in bulk bales.
  • Stock Lot Buyers: Procuring premium-grade cancelled apparel and home textile production stocks.

Our Services

Comprehensive solutions tailored for international buyers.

Premier Exporter

What It Is: Exporting custom-manufactured apparel and home textiles from Pakistan to global markets under GOTS, BCI, and OCS standards.

What's Included: Custom pattern drafting, fabric sourcing, labeling, pre-shipment AQL 1.5 inspection, and sea/air freight booking.

Who It's For: International apparel brands, retail chains, and textile distributors in Europe, North America, and UAE.

How It Works: We receive your tech pack, source materials, create samples, execute bulk production, and manage direct container exports.

Exporter of Fresh Stock Lots

What It Is: Procurement and export of fresh, custom-manufactured clothing production stocks direct from high-compliance Pakistani mills.

What's Included: Immediate stock verification, size sorting, custom repackaging, and clean export documentation clearance.

Who It's For: Wholesale distributors, discount stores, and bulk buyers seeking premium stock lots at lower MOQs.

How It Works: We source available factory stock, run pre-purchase inspection checks, repackage, and dispatch container shipments.

Cancellation Sourcing

What It Is: Exporting high-value cancelled shipment stocks of apparel, home textiles, and cotton cleaning rags at steep clearance discounts.

What's Included: Sourcing cancelled lots, complete defect checks, button/trim alterations, metal detection scanning, and packaging.

Who It's For: Off-price retail stores, industrial cleaning companies, and high-volume value retailers.

How It Works: We identify cancelled stock lots in Karachi, verify the inventory count and condition, and handle fast export clearance.

Sourcing & Buying Agent

What It Is: On-ground representative services in Pakistan, managing factories, price negotiations, and QA inspections on your behalf.

What's Included: Factory auditing, cost breakdown checks, daily sampling progress tracking, and on-site AQL 1.5/2.5 final inspections.

Who It's For: Brand owners and foreign importers who want to manufacture in Pakistan without traveling or dealing with factories directly.

How It Works: Send us your specs; we vet factories, supervise the entire manufacturing process, inspect, and approve shipment.
