Knitwear and fleece garments, including hoodies, crewneck sweatshirts, joggers, and activewear, represent some of Pakistan's highest-volume textile exports. With a rich history of cotton knitting and processing, Pakistan's manufacturers cater to leading global brands, e-commerce giants, and promotional merchandise companies. Sourcing knitwear from this region offers brands access to exceptional fabric softness, reliable colorfastness, and highly competitive pricing.

In this guide, we analyze the key aspects of sourcing fleece and knitwear from Pakistan, focusing on fabric types, weight specifications, and production compliance.

## 1. Popular Knitwear Fabric Types

Pakistan's knitting mills produce a diverse range of knit fabrics to suit different seasons and branding requirements:
- **Fleece (Brushed/Unbrushed)**: Typically a blend of cotton and polyester (e.g., 80/20 or 60/40), brushed on the inside to create a soft, warm nap. Unbrushed fleece (French Terry) is ideal for lightweight, trans-seasonal activewear.
- **Single Jersey**: The standard lightweight fabric for t-shirts, tank tops, and innerwear, available in 100% cotton or poly-cotton stretch blends.
- **Interlock & Rib Knits**: Double-knit fabrics that offer a smooth surface on both sides, providing excellent shape retention and stretch, ideal for high-end polo shirts and cuffs.

## 2. Fabric Weight (GSM) and Yarn Counts

Specifying the correct weight and yarn thickness is critical to achieving the desired drape and hand-feel:
- **Lightweight (140-180 GSM)**: Best for standard t-shirts and summer activewear.
- **Midweight (240-280 GSM)**: Standard weight for trans-seasonal hoodies, crewnecks, and light joggers.
- **Heavyweight (320-400+ GSM)**: Premium, dense fabric weights for oversized hoodies, streetwear collections, and cold-weather fleece outerwear.
- **Yarn Counts**: Selecting the right yarn (e.g., 20s or 30s singles) affects fabric smoothness and screen-printing clarity.

## 3. Embellishments and Printing Techniques

Knitwear often serves as a canvas for branding. Factories in Pakistan offer a wide array of high-quality embellishment services:
- **Screen Printing**: Plastisol, water-based, and discharge printing for durable, high-density graphics.
- **Embroidery**: High-speed multi-head computerized embroidery for chest logos, appliques, and chenille patches.
- **Custom Dyeing**: Reactive dyeing processes that ensure rich color saturation and high colorfastness to washing.

## 4. Quality Audits and Compliance Standards

To protect your brand's reputation, verify that your knitwear manufacturer complies with global industry standards:
- **Social Compliance**: Ensure the factory is BSCI, WRAP, or Sedex audited to guarantee fair wages, safe working environments, and zero child labor.
- **Chemical Safety**: Request fabric compliance certificates (such as REACH or OEKO-TEX Standard 100) to ensure that no harmful dyes or chemicals are used in the production process.
- **Shrinkage testing**: Conduct pre-shipment tests to verify that fabric shrinkage remains within standard limits (+/- 5%) after commercial laundering.
