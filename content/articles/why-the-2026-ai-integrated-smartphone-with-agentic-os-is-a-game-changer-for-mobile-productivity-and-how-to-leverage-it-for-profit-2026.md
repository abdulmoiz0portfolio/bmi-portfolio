<h3>Can the AI integrated smartphone 2026 operate completely offline?</h3>
<p>Yes. Thanks to the on‑device NPUs and the locally stored Agentic OS LLMs, most productivity tasks—speech‑to‑text, image analysis, document summarization—run without an internet connection. Cloud fallback is only used for ultra‑large model queries or software updates.</p>

<h3>How does Agentic OS protect my personal data?</h3>
<p>Agentic OS employs a zero‑knowledge architecture: all raw data stays encrypted in the device’s Secure Enclave. When learning from user interactions, the system uses federated learning, sending only model gradients (not raw data) to the vendor’s servers, ensuring compliance with GDPR, CCPA, and emerging AI‑ethics regulations.</p>
