<h2>FAQ – Frequently Asked Questions About AI Edge Smartphones in 2026</h2>

<h3>What exactly is an NPU and how does it differ from a GPU?</h3>
<p>An NPU (Neural Processing Unit) is a silicon accelerator designed specifically for matrix‑multiplication and tensor operations that power AI models. Unlike GPUs, which handle a broader range of graphics tasks, NPUs are optimized for low‑latency, low‑power inference, making them ideal for real‑time on‑device AI.</p>

<h3>Can I run desktop‑class AI models on my phone?</h3>
<p>Yes, but with caveats. Models need to be quantized and pruned to fit the device’s memory constraints. Frameworks like TensorFlow Lite 3.0 and PyTorch Mobile now provide automated tools to convert large models into edge‑friendly versions without major accuracy loss.</p>

<h3>Is my data safe when using on‑device AI assistants?</h3>
<p>Because the inference runs locally, raw data never leaves the handset. Agentic OS encrypts model weights and uses a secure enclave for any temporary data, aligning with GDPR and CCPA requirements.</p>

<h3>Do AI edge smartphones drain the battery faster?</h3>
<p>Modern NPUs are incredibly power‑efficient—most high‑end models consume under 2 W at peak. When paired with AI‑aware power management (e.g., “AI Battery Dashboard”), overall battery life can even improve because fewer background network requests are needed.</p>
